Is the pill right for me?

Taken every day, the pill is a very effective way to prevent pregnancy. Many people also take it for painful or heavy periods, irregular periods, PMS or acne.

Combined pills are not suitable for everyone. The doctor may suggest the mini-pill or another option if, for example, you:

  • are 35 or older and smoke
  • have migraines with aura
  • have had a blood clot (thrombosis), or have high blood pressure
  • are breastfeeding, or gave birth recently

The pill does not protect against sexually transmitted infections. For STD testing, you're welcome to visit our clinic.

Side effects

In the first two or three months, some people have nausea, spotting between periods, breast tenderness or headaches. These usually settle as your body gets used to the pill.

A rare but serious side effect of combined pills is a blood clot. Seek medical care right away if you have severe pain or swelling in one leg, chest pain, sudden shortness of breath, a sudden severe headache, or problems with your vision or speech.

Questions, answered

Do I need Japanese health insurance?

No. Birth control pills are not covered by Japanese health insurance, so the price is the same for everyone, whether you live in Japan or are visiting.

I'm visiting Japan. Can you deliver to my hotel?

Yes. We deliver to hotels, Airbnbs and other places to stay anywhere in Japan. Enter the address and your name as the recipient on the payment page. We can't deliver to addresses outside Japan.

Do I need to visit the clinic?

No. First visits can be online too. If you'd rather be seen in person, you can walk in during opening hours without an appointment.

Is there a female doctor?

Our doctors are male, and an English speaking female staff member is on every call. Please let us know if you have any concerns, and we will do our best to make you comfortable.

How do I get a refill?

Book an online visit the same way, and choose "Refill of a medication prescribed here before" in the questionnaire. Online repeat prescriptions have no consultation fee, so you pay only for the pill and shipping.

My pill from home isn't on your list. Can I still get it?

Tell us its name. The same hormones are often sold under different brand names in Japan, and the doctor will suggest the same or the closest match.

Do you also prescribe the morning-after pill?

Will anyone know what I ordered?

No. Orders ship in discreet, unbranded packaging, and your personal and medical information is kept confidential.
