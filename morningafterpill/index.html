How it works

The morning-after pill contains levonorgestrel, the same hormone as Plan B. It mainly works by delaying ovulation. It is not an abortion pill, and it will not end a pregnancy that has already started.

You may want it if a condom broke or slipped off, you missed birth control pills, you had sex without protection, or you were forced to have sex.

If you were sexually assaulted, please come to us or to the nearest clinic as soon as you can. You can also call Japan's nationwide sexual violence helpline at #8891.

After you take it

  • Some people feel nauseous or have a headache, usually for about a day.
  • If you vomit within 2 hours of taking the pill, contact us. You may need another dose.
  • Your next period may come earlier or later than usual.
  • If your period is more than a week late, take a pregnancy test.
  • The morning-after pill does not protect you for the rest of your cycle. Use condoms until your next period, or start a regular method such as the pill.

Questions, answered

Do I need an appointment?

No. Just walk in during opening hours. We're open 7 days a week, including weekends and national holidays. Please check our closed days before you come.

Do I need Japanese health insurance?

No. The morning-after pill is not covered by Japanese health insurance, so the price is the same for everyone, whether you live in Japan or are visiting.

I'm under 18. Can I still get it?

Yes. There is no age limit, and you don't need a parent with you or a consent form.

Is there an internal exam?

No. You have a short consultation with the doctor, with no internal exam and no need to undress.

It has been more than 72 hours. Is it too late?

Please come in or book a visit anyway, and talk to the doctor about your situation as soon as you can.

Can I get ella (ulipristal) in Japan?

Ulipristal is not available in Japan. We prescribe levonorgestrel, the same hormone as Plan B.

Does it affect my future fertility?

No. The morning-after pill does not affect your ability to get pregnant in the future.

Is there a female doctor?

Our doctors are male, and English speaking female staff are there to help you. Please let us know if you have any concerns, and we will do our best to make you comfortable.

Will anyone know?

No. Your personal and medical information is kept confidential. Online orders ship in discreet, unbranded packaging.
