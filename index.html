How it works

Five steps. Zero waiting rooms.

01

Book online

Pick any open slot on our English booking page, it takes about a minute. Your confirmation email and Zoom link arrive straight away. See available times.

02

Meet the doctor on Zoom

Join from your phone or laptop, no account, no app to set up, just click the link. An English speaking staff member is on the call alongside the doctor. See how a Zoom visit works.

03

Your invoice arrives by email

After the consultation we send you an invoice by email through Square, our payment provider. It lists exactly what you're being charged for, nothing is charged from your card before your confirmation.

04

Pay by card

Open the invoice and pay online with a credit card. No cash, no bank transfer, no queue at a register, and no Japanese bank account needed.

05

Your medication is delivered

As soon as your payment goes through, your prescription is packaged and sent, often the very next day, to your home, your hotel, or wherever you're staying in Japan. Your parcel is treated with care and privacy as a standard policy.
